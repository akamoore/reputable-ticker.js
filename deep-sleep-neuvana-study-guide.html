About the Study

53 people used the Neuvana Xen device every day for 6 weeks. Everyone wore an Oura Ring to track sleep and filled out surveys at the start, midpoint, and end. 36 participants completed the full study with complete data.

Key Takeaways

🚶More Daily Movement

Participants walked about 10% more per day on average — roughly 650 extra steps — and were more active overall. Three out of four people moved more during the study than they had at baseline.

🌙Tighter Nights

Participants spent less time lying awake in bed. Sleep became more consolidated — fewer interruptions, a more settled night — especially for the men in the study.

⚖️A Steady System

Heart rate and heart rate variability stayed in their normal range throughout. The practice shifted how people moved and slept, without putting stress on the cardiovascular system.
