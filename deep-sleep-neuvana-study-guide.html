What this study is about.

Neuvana Xen is a pair of earbuds that sends a very gentle electrical pulse through your ears to stimulate the vagus nerve — the nerve that helps your body shift into a calmer, more rested state. Early research suggests it might help with sleep and stress.

This study asks a simple question: if you use the device every day for six weeks, does your sleep actually change? We'll measure sleep two ways — through your Oura ring (which tracks sleep stages, heart rate, and recovery overnight) and through your own answers on short surveys at three points during the study.

You don't need to be a researcher. You don't need to change your life. You just need to keep your usual routines, use the device once a day, and answer a few questions when we ask. That's it.

Your daily routine.

Three simple things. None take long.

i

Use Neuvana Xen once a day

Follow the instructions that came with your device. Most people use it in the evening, before bed — but you can pick the time that works for you, as long as it's consistent.

ii

Wear your Oura ring every day and every night

Keep the ring on your finger as much as possible — including while you sleep. Sync it with the Reputable Health platform once a day or so. That's how we'll see your sleep data.

iii

Complete each survey when it arrives

You'll get a short survey at the start, at day 21, and at day 42. Each one takes about 5–10 minutes. Answer honestly — there's no "right" answer.

What we're measuring.

Two sources of information: your ring, and your own words.

From your Oura ring

Automatic · Nightly

Total sleep, how long it takes you to fall asleep, time spent in deep sleep and REM, heart rate, and heart rate variability. You don't have to do anything — just wear the ring and let it sync.

From your surveys

3 short check-ins

How you feel you've been sleeping, how often certain things woke you up, how rested you feel in the morning, and whether you noticed any effect from the device. Your experience matters as much as the data.

Keeping things clean.

So we can tell what's actually the device versus something else, please try to keep the rest of your life steady for the 6 weeks.

  • Keep your usual sleep schedule. No big shifts in bedtime or wake time if you can help it.
  • Keep your usual exercise routine. Don't start or stop an exercise program during the study.
  • Don't start new sleep/stress/mood medications or supplements (prescription or over-the-counter) while the study is running.
  • Don't worry if a night goes sideways — one bad night won't ruin the study. Just keep going.
  • Don't push through if something feels wrong. If the device ever feels uncomfortable or you want out, just let us know.

Your privacy.

Your data stays private. When we write up what we found at the end of the study, we'll only ever share averages across all participants — never anything traceable back to you. Your name, email, and personal details are kept separate from your sleep data and survey answers. Only the research team sees the underlying data.

If at any point you want to stop the study, remove your data, or ask us a question about how we're handling it, you can — no explanation needed.

We're here. The research team reads every message — usually within a business day.
