Key insights

What the data suggests

🌙 REM sleep saw the largest positive shift

Your REM sleep increased by 22.53%, translating to an additional 16.64 minutes per night on average. REM sleep is critical for emotional regulation, memory consolidation, and cognitive recovery — a meaningful improvement during just 7 days of retreat activities.

❤️ Average heart rate dropped significantly

Your average heart rate decreased by 12.76% (down 12.54 bpm). A lower resting heart rate is generally associated with improved cardiovascular fitness and reduced stress — suggesting the retreat's combination of movement, breathwork, and recovery modalities had a measurable calming effect on your system.

⏰ You spent 14% less time awake at night

Time awake during sleep dropped by 14.06% (-13.24 minutes). Combined with the increase in REM sleep, this suggests your sleep architecture shifted toward more restorative patterns. You were spending less time restless and more time in the deep stages that matter most for recovery.

📊 A note on statistical significance

None of the metrics reached statistical significance in this report. This is expected with a short 7-day study window and individual-level data. The trends are directionally promising — a longer study period or larger group would provide more statistical power to confirm these patterns.
